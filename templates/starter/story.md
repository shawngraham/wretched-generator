# My Wretched Game

Introduce your character and setting here.

## How to Play

1. **Roll the turn dice** to see how many cards to draw.
2. **Draw the cards** and read each prompt.
3. **Write your response** in the journal, then apply the card's effects.
4. Keep going until the deck is empty (you survive) or you lose.

## Winning and Losing

Explain what it means to survive, and what each way of losing looks like.
